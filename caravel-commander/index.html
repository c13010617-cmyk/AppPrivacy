Caravel Commander Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Caravel Commander handles information when you use the app.

Information handled by the app

Caravel Commander does not require an account and does not ask you to provide your name, email address, postal address, telephone number, or other directly identifying information to use the game.

Advertising

Caravel Commander uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) to display advertisements, including banner and rewarded advertisements. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, approximate location, advertising and app interaction information, and other information used to provide, measure, prevent abuse of, and personalize advertising, subject to your choices and applicable law.

Where consent is required, Caravel Commander uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to present privacy and consent choices before requesting ads where required. You can manage available advertising and privacy choices through the consent options presented by the app.

Apple App Tracking Transparency

Where applicable, Caravel Commander may request permission under Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework before allowing tracking for advertising purposes. If you deny permission, the app respects that choice.

Third-party services

Advertising and consent services are provided by Google. Their processing is governed by their own privacy policies and terms.

Data retention and deletion

Caravel Commander does not maintain a user account or a separate developer-operated profile of players. Information processed by third-party advertising providers is subject to those providers' retention and deletion practices and applicable law.

Children

Caravel Commander is not directed specifically at children under the age applicable to the relevant jurisdiction. The app does not knowingly request directly identifying information from children.

Changes to this policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the app's functionality, advertising services, or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published at this URL with a new “Last updated” date.

Contact

For privacy questions concerning Caravel Commander, please use the contact information provided on the app's official App Store listing or the developer's official support channel.
